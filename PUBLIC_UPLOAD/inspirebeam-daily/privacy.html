InspireBeam Daily
Privacy Policy

How InspireBeam Daily handles information and your privacy choices.

Effective date: October 18, 2026
App: InspireBeam Daily (com.jvcdigitalsolutions.inspirebeam)
Developer: JVC Digital Solutions
Privacy contact: jvcdigitalsolutions@gmail.com

1. About the app and its audience

InspireBeam Daily is a Bible devotional application for people aged 13 and older. It includes English and Filipino Bible verses, 30 devotional readings that repeat in rotation, reflections, prayers, favorites, reading history, local reminders, optional banner advertisements, and an optional Google Play purchase to remove the app's advertisements. Devotional reading is available offline. The app is not intended or marketed for children under 13. People aged 13–17 may receive additional protections according to applicable laws and platform policies.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your language preference, text size, reminder settings, favorites, reading history, first-use date, and a locally cached ad-free purchase status in Android app-private storage. Its Bible verses and devotional content are included with the app.

The reviewed version does not operate a JVC Digital Solutions user-account system or upload your favorites or devotional reading history to a JVC Digital Solutions server. Android's normal app-data backup is disabled in the reviewed configuration.

3. Advertising and information processed by Google

The free version integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for banner advertisements. When advertising services run, Google's SDK automatically collects and shares certain data for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. This may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • interactions with the app, including app launches and taps;
  • diagnostics and performance information, such as load times, hangs and device performance; and
  • device or account-related identifiers, such as advertising identifiers where available.

The app integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent notices and privacy choices when required. Available choices depend on region and Google's consent configuration. The app's advertising configuration requests TEEN age-restricted treatment and G-rated maximum ad content for all ad requests, including adult users. These settings are intended to limit ad personalization and content, but do not replace other region-specific legal safeguards.

See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can also manage advertising choices through applicable Google and device settings.

4. Purchases and payments

InspireBeam Daily offers an optional one-time Lifetime Ad-Free purchase using Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment transaction. The app receives information needed to check purchase and entitlement status and may save an ad-free status locally so that a previously established entitlement can remain available offline. The app does not request or store your credit-card number or bank account details. Records managed by Google Play remain subject to Google's privacy practices.

The app's purchase experience and entitlement verification may change in future versions. If a developer-operated purchase verification service is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain any new information it receives.

5. Reminders and sharing

If you enable daily reminders and grant any required Android notification permission, notifications are scheduled locally. This app does not use its own cloud push-notification service in the reviewed version. Sharing a verse or devotional is optional: Android lets you choose another app to receive the selected content, and the receiving app has its own privacy terms.

6. Why information is used and who handles it

Local settings support offline reading, favorites, history and reminders. Google and its applicable advertising partners may use information for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Google Play processes purchase-related information. JVC Digital Solutions does not maintain a cloud database of users' devotional reading history. The reviewed Android app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise GPS location permissions.

Third-party providers may process information outside your country according to their own privacy notices and applicable law.

7. Retention, deletion and privacy requests

Locally saved preferences and reading information remain on your device until they are changed or removed. You can remove favorites in the app, disable reminders in Settings, and remove locally saved app data through your Android device's Settings → Apps → InspireBeam Daily → Storage → Clear data option or by uninstalling the app. Clearing the app's data does not delete purchase records, advertising records, or other data held separately by Google and third parties.

To exercise privacy rights or ask about data processing by JVC Digital Solutions, email jvcdigitalsolutions@gmail.com. If you email us, your address and the information in your message will be processed to respond to your inquiry. You may request deletion of communications you have sent us, subject to applicable legal or operational retention requirements. For data managed by Google, see Google Account controls and Google's privacy information.

8. Security

Local preferences are stored in Android app-private storage. The app disables ordinary cleartext HTTP traffic in its configuration. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts its transferred data in transit with Transport Layer Security (TLS). No online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated when the app, advertising, purchasing, privacy controls or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised to reflect a substantive update.

10. Contact

For support and privacy questions, contact JVC Digital Solutions at jvcdigitalsolutions@gmail.com.
